## PostgreSQL Safety Reference

Bitnami's postgresql chart major version tracks the PostgreSQL major version:

| Chart Series | PostgreSQL Major |
|-------------|-----------------|
| 15.x | PG 15 |
| 16.x | PG 16 |
| 17.x | PG 17 |
| 18.x | PG 18 |

**frolf-postgres** is pinned to the 16.x series (PG16) and must NOT be updated to 17.x, 18.x, or any chart that ships a different PostgreSQL major version without:
1. Creating a full data backup
2. Running a `pg_upgrade` or dump/restore migration
3. Scheduling a maintenance window with downtime

**resume-postgres** was updated within the 18.x series (PG18 → PG18). No initdb risk.
